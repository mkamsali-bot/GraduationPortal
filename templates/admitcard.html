Instructions

  1. Carry a printed or digital copy of your Admit Card for QR code scanning at the entrance.
  2. Present your Admit Card at the entrance for verification.
  3. Helipad Arena is the common venue for the Graduation Ceremony for all Graduands.
  4. Parents/Accompanying Guests will be allotted separate seating at the Helipad Arena, Shivaji Auditorium, or D Block (Level 1) Classrooms, as indicated on the Admit Card. Live streaming of the Graduation Ceremony will be available at the designated venue.
  5. Degree Certificates will be distributed at the following venues:
    • Helipad Arena: GSCSE, GSCE, GSA, GSP and GSHS.
    • Shivaji Auditorium: GSB and GSS.
  6. Graduands of GSB and GSS are requested to note that their Admit Card contains details of both the Graduation Ceremony Venue (Helipad Arena) and the Degree Certificate Collection Venue (Shivaji Auditorium). Kindly follow the venue details indicated on the Admit Card.
  7. Please be seated in your allotted seat by 2:00 PM.
  8. Retain your Admit Card until the conclusion of the Graduation Ceremony.
  9. The Admit Card is non-transferable.
  10. Kindly follow all instructions issued by the University officials and volunteers throughout the ceremony.
